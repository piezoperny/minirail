Mini Rail Controls & Supply Chain

  • Build Tracks: Click & drag from any station (or line T-bar end) to another station. New lines auto-dispatch an idle matching locomotive!
  • Supply Chain & Growth: Haul Raw Materials from Resource Sites (Forests/Mines) to Factories to craft Goods, then deliver Goods to Hamlets & Towns to grow them into Cities (+1 bonus upgrade!).
  • Passenger Service: Express trains carry Commuters (Towns → Cities), Local Travelers (Cities → Towns), and color-coded Intercity Tickets between Cities.
  • Consists & +Carriage: Every locomotive spawns with 2 coupled carriages (6 capacity). Drag +Carriage onto any car of a train for +3 capacity (up to 4 carriages).
  • Terrain & Upgrades: Drag Double-Track or Tunnel tokens onto segments; river crossings auto-use Bridges. Right-click a train or segment to reclaim/remove.
