Mini Rail Controls

  • Build Tracks: Click & drag from any station (or line T-bar end) to another station.
  • Insert / Delete: Drag a segment onto a station to insert it; drag a T-bar back to its previous station (or right-click a segment) to remove.
  • Trains & Upgrades: Drag Express, Freight, Railcar, Double-Track, or Tunnel tokens from the bottom dock onto tracks or trains. Drag existing trains/upgrades to redeploy.
  • Single-Track & Terrain: Stations act as passing sidings. Crossing rivers uses a Bridge; hills slow Expresses (-40%) unless you add a Tunnel.
  • Shapes: Villages (■) ↔ Cities (●) for Express; Resources (▲) → Ports (◆) for Freight.
