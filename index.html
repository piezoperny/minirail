Mini Rail Controls & Supply Chain

  • Build Tracks: Drag from any station or line T-bar end to another station. Right-click a station to bypass/remove it; right-click a line pill to clear the route.
  • Camera Controls: Scroll or pinch to zoom (`0.45×–2.5×`). Drag empty paper, middle-drag, or hold Space+drag to pan. Press F or 0 to fit map, N for Night Mode.
  • Supply Chain & Growth: Haul Raw Materials from Resource Sites (Forests/Mines) to Factories to craft Goods, then deliver Goods to Hamlets & Towns to grow them into Cities.
  • Passenger Service: Express trains carry Commuters (Towns → Cities), Local Travelers (Cities → Towns), and color-coded Intercity Tickets between Cities.
  • Consists & Upgrades: Drag +Carriage onto any car of a train, Double-Track or Tunnel onto segments, or Interchange onto a busy station.
