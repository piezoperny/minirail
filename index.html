Mini Rail Controls & Supply Chain

  • One-Stroke & Pull-Out Lines: Drag across stations in one continuous stroke to connect them. Pull a line’s T-bar end backward to shorten it, or pull an interior station outward to bypass or reroute it. Right-click a line pill to clear a route.
  • Passenger vs. Freight: Passenger bullet trains carry Commuters (Towns ↔ Cities) and color-coded Intercity Tickets. Freight dark-framed hoppers haul Raw Materials (Forest/Mine → Factory) and Goods (Factory → Hamlet/Town) to grow Cities.
  • Rivers, Mountains & Hills: Bridges auto-span rivers/lakes and Tunnels auto-bore through Mountains (reclaimed when tracks are removed). Drag Tunnel onto rolling hills for full speed, Double-Track for passing, +Carriage onto trains, or Interchange onto hubs.
  • Camera & Shortcuts: Scroll or pinch to zoom (0.45×–2.5×). Drag empty paper, middle-drag, or Space+drag to pan. Press F/0 to fit map, N for Night Mode, M to mute, Space to pause.
